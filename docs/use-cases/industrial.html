Industrial

Maintenance, quality and supply-chain decisions where the SLM reads technician notes and the rulebook is 400 pages.

On this page

Industrial decisions rarely arrive as clean text. They arrive as a technician's note, a work order, a sensor alarm, and a maintenance manual. The SLM's job is to join those into a structured decision: fix now, schedule, monitor, or escalate.

Spec and policy

spec = DecisionSpec(
    name="work_order_triage",
    actions=["immediate", "next_shift", "planned", "monitor"],
    costs={
        ("immediate",  "critical"): 0,        ("immediate",  "minor"): 18_000,
        ("next_shift", "critical"): 120_000,  ("next_shift", "minor"): 2_000,
        ("planned",    "critical"): 260_000,  ("planned",    "minor"): 0,
        ("monitor",    "critical"): 480_000,  ("monitor",    "minor"): 0,
    },
    abstain="next_shift",
)

Reading the manual: retrieval is mandatory

Plant rules live in PDFs that change quarterly. Retrieval over the maintenance manual is what makes the decision correct rather than merely plausible:

retriever = Retriever(index="maintenance-manual", embed_model="BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5", top_k=4)
policy = HybridPolicy(spec, slm=slm, retriever=retriever,
                      template="Relevant procedure:\n{context}\n\nFault report:\n{query}\n\nPriority?")
# keep retrieved context <= ~40% of the 2k window; beyond that small models get worse

Edge deployment reality

Plant networks are air-gapped and often offline. A 360M INT8 model (~360 MB) fits a fanless industrial PC with 4 GB RAM; a 66M encoder fits a Raspberry Pi 4 in under 200 MB. Sync the model artefact on a USB or an internal registry — plan for the update path before you plan for the model.

  • Model + tokenizer + calibration bundled as one signed artefact, verified on load.
  • Graceful degradation: if the model fails to load, fall back to the deterministic rule table.
  • Local logging with store-and-forward; never block a decision on network availability.
  • Rollback in one command, offline.

Human factors

Planners override models they do not trust, and they are usually right to. Show the model's suggestion with the manual excerpt it used; override rates drop sharply when the evidence is visible, and every override is a free label for the next training round.
