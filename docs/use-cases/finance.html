Finance

Fraud, credit and KYC decisions under hard latency budgets and an auditor who will ask why.

On this page

Finance is the best-documented commercial home for SLM decision models: high volume, closed label sets, asymmetric costs, strict latency and a regulator who wants explanations. All five of those push toward small, self-hosted, deterministic models.

Decision inventory

The cost matrix is the model

spec = DecisionSpec(
    name="chargeback_triage",
    actions=["auto_resolve", "investigate", "escalate"],
    costs={
        ("auto_resolve", "fraudulent"): 45_000,   # paid out on fraud
        ("auto_resolve", "legitimate"): 0,
        ("investigate",  "fraudulent"): 1_200,    # analyst hour
        ("investigate",  "legitimate"): 1_200,
        ("escalate",     "fraudulent"): 6_500,    # senior + legal
        ("escalate",     "legitimate"): 6_500,
    },
    abstain="investigate", max_abstain_rate=0.35,
)

With a 37:1 ratio between a bad auto-resolve and an investigation, the optimal policy investigates anything above ~2.6% fraud probability — a far wider band than intuition suggests. Publishing that derivation is what makes the policy defensible in an audit.

Explainability that satisfies a reviewer

Not SHAP waterfalls — reviewers want the specific evidence. Make the SLM emit a reason code from a closed set and store the evidence spans:

{"action": "investigate",
 "confidence": 0.68,
 "reason_code": "R7",                 # R7 = merchant category mismatch
 "evidence": ["MCC 5999 vs stated 5411", "3 prior disputes in 90 days"],
 "model": "slm-360m-chargeback@v4", "prompt_hash": "9f2c…", "calib_T": 1.31}

Fairness and adverse action

A decline is an adverse action. In most jurisdictions it requires a stated reason, and in India, RBI's digital lending and fair-practices directions constrain automated decisioning. Practical controls:

  • Never let the SLM see protected attributes directly; test for disparate impact on proxy variables.
  • Track approval rate by slice monthly, with a documented threshold for investigation (e.g. ±5 points).
  • Keep a counterfactual review set: 2% of auto-decisions are human-reviewed regardless of score.
  • Retain inputs, scores and decisions for the statutory period — often 5–8 years.

Latency engineering

Sub-50 ms decisions cannot afford a 360M decoder in the hot path. The pattern that works: pre-compute SLM features for slow-moving entities (merchant, customer, device) offline, then let the real-time model consume them.

offline  : SLM(merchant_profile)  -> embedding + risk tag, refreshed nightly
realtime : gradient_boosted(txn_features, slm_features)  -> action,   p99 18 ms

This keeps the SLM's judgement without its latency, and it is the most common production pattern in payments.
